01.B / FIELD DIRECTIVES FOUR RULES OF SECTOR 9

How it plays

Directive 01

Read the threat

Every hostile has its own awareness, and the threat meter on your HUD climbs as they notice you. Break line of sight and it settles. Push your luck and the whole squad turns.

Threat Hidden
  1. Hidden
  2. Suspicious
  3. Searching
  4. Detected
  5. Combat

Unaware. Patrol routes unchanged — they have not seen you.

Website demonstration of the five in-game threat states.

Directive 02

Move without a sound

Crouch to stay low, pick your angle and slip behind a hostile who hasn’t seen you. A silent takedown drops them without a shot — and the rest of the squad is none the wiser.

  • Crouch lowers your profile
  • Takedowns from behind, on unaware targets
  • No noise, no reinforcements

Directive 03

Control the engagement

Health, stamina and ammunition all sit on your HUD. Sprinting burns stamina. Every magazine runs dry — reload behind cover, not in the open.

HP ST
VK-77 “Vandal” 30 / 120 Reloading

Directive 04

Survive the boss

Every fifth stage the district sends something heavier: one named enemy carrying a heavy weapon, with far more health than any soldier before it. Use all of the cover.

Hurt hostiles fall back to regroup — don’t let them. And if you go down, you can choose to watch a short video and get back up once per deployment, with your stage and progress intact.

04 / SYSTEM SPECIFICATIONS

Tactical combat. Built for your phone.

Designed around a touch screen and a mid-range Android phone, not ported to them.

  • SPEC.01

    60 FPS target

    Every graphics tier targets 60 frames per second on supported phones, and resolution scales itself to hold it.

  • SPEC.02

    Adaptive graphics

    Low, Medium, High and Ultra presets, tuned automatically so budget phones and flagships both play smoothly.

    LOWMEDHIGHULTRA

  • SPEC.03

    Haptics & interface audio

    Springy menus with tactile sound and optional vibration on every press. Switch vibration off in Settings any time.

  • SPEC.04

    Seven languages

    The game is fully playable in:

    • EN
    • TR
    • DE
    • ES
    • RU
    • AR
    • HI

  • SPEC.05

    Daily rewards

    Claim a reward each day you play and build a streak.

  • SPEC.06

    Offline-friendly

    The campaign plays without a connection and without an account. Optional rewarded videos need one.

  • SPEC.07

    Small download

    Operators, weapons and effects are drawn in code instead of shipped as textures, so the install stays light.

  • SPEC.08

    Touch-first controls

    Twin-stick touch controls with jump, crouch, reload, weapon swap, takedown and pause. No controller required — though gamepads work too.
