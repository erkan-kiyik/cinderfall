KOR — the city is Vanta's. The night is ours.

The foundry's furnace has been cold for four years. Power, bread and curfew now come from Vanta: a private firm that took over the city's "temporary" security and gets its contract extended every time it runs out. The Kor line is five people: a welder, a firefighter, a signals analyst who walked out of Vanta, a former courier and a dock worker. All they have is each other.

KOR is a 2D tactical shooter made for phones, painted by hand in look and feel.

■ FIVE OPERATORS, FIFTEEN ABILITIES
FLINT, WARDEN, VESPER, SKYE and BULWARK, each with three abilities from the life they had before: a fire door, a smoke canister, a radio jammer, a drone made from scrap, a shield cut from a container door… Pick your operator, build your loadout.

■ A CITY THAT LIVES
The port gate, the morning market, the governor's square. Rain, street lamps mirrored in wet asphalt, bare bulbs off a generator, fires in barrels. The people's yellow light against Vanta's clean white.

■ TACTICAL COMBAT
Take cover and pick your moment. Vanta troopers patrol, investigate noise and change cover; the shield man pushes forward. Play reckless and you fall fast.

■ CAMPAIGN AND ENDLESS MODE
The first act of the Kor line campaign: three missions, each with story scenes before and after. In endless mode the waves get harder and every fifth brings Secretary Gall. New tasks every day.

■ WEAPONS, CRATES, HAWK
Twelve weapons, from pistols and shotguns to marksman rifles and machine guns. Weapon finishes in crates seized from Vanta; trade with HAWK, the oldest dealer in the market.

■ OFFLINE
Single player; no connection needed. Turkish and English.
